About

I'm a final-year B.Tech student in Electronics and Communication Engineering at A. D. Patel Institute of Technology, graduating in 2026.

My focus is on RTL design — writing clean, synthesizable Verilog — and physical design, taking that RTL through the full ASIC backend: floorplanning, placement, clock tree synthesis, routing, and DRC sign-off.

I work with open-source EDA tools (OpenROAD, Magic VLSI, KLayout) on the sky130A PDK and prefer understanding every TCL command and tool invocation rather than relying on automated wrappers.
